# life-science-mcp

A unified MCP server for 127 life science APIs and databases.

## Installation

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "life-science": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "life-science-mcp"]
    }
  }
}
```

## Example Queries

### Beginner

| Category | Query |
|----------|-------|
| Gene lookup | *What is the approved gene symbol for p53, what chromosome is it on, and what are its known protein domains?* |
| Chemistry | *Give me the molecular formula, weight, canonical SMILES for metformin, and find its RxNorm drug concept with related formulations.* |
| Functional annotation | *What biological process GO terms are annotated to the human insulin receptor gene INSR, and what orthologous groups does it belong to in EggNOG?* |
| Clinical trials | *Find open clinical trials recruiting patients with glioblastoma and check which drugs in those trials have known targets in DGIdb.* |
| Taxonomy | *What species are in the family Felidae, how are they classified in the NCBI taxonomy, and when did cats and dogs diverge according to TimeTree?* |
| Structural biology | *Retrieve the AlphaFold-predicted structure confidence (pLDDT) for human serum albumin and check if it has any intrinsically disordered regions in MobiDB.* |
| Pharmacovigilance | *What are the known side effects of ibuprofen in FDA adverse event reports, and what does its DailyMed label say about hepatotoxicity warnings?* |
| Pathways | *Look up the glycolysis pathway in KEGG, list the enzymes involved, and find which of those enzymes have solved crystal structures in PDB.* |
| Single-cell | *What single-cell RNA-seq datasets are available for human lung tissue in CELLxGENE?* |
| Variant annotation | *What are the predicted functional effects of the BRAF V600E mutation using Ensembl VEP, and what is its clinical significance in ClinVar?* |

### Intermediate

| Category | Query |
|----------|-------|
| Pharmacogenomics | *My patient had an unexpected bleeding event on standard-dose warfarin — are there CYP2C9 or VKORC1 variants that could explain abnormal metabolism, what are their allele frequencies in gnomAD, and do PharmGKB clinical dosing guidelines exist for those genotypes?* |
| Drug metabolism | *CYP3A4 metabolizes over half of all prescribed drugs — which of its protein interaction partners in STRING are also drug-metabolizing enzymes, what are their tissue expression patterns in GTEx, and do any share substrates that could cause undocumented interactions according to RxNorm?* |
| Post-GWAS | *A GWAS hit near FOXP3 is associated with autoimmune disease but the mechanism is unknown — what regulatory elements overlap this locus in ENCODE, what pathways is FOXP3 in via Reactome and Pathway Commons, and are there phenotypically similar rare diseases caused by FOXP3 mutations in Orphanet?* |
| Drug repurposing | *Imatinib was designed for BCR-ABL, but what other kinase targets does it hit according to ChEMBL and Open Targets, are any of those targets implicated in non-cancer diseases via DisGeNET, and are there trials on ClinicalTrials.gov testing imatinib for those conditions?* |
| Evolutionary biology | *The FOXP2 "language gene" is highly conserved — compare its orthologs across human, mouse, zebrafish, and chicken using OMA, check domain architecture in InterPro, estimate divergence times with TimeTree, and look for lineage-specific annotations in OrthoDB and EggNOG.* |
| Marine ecology | *Lionfish (Pterois volitans) are invasive in the Caribbean — where are they observed in GBIF and OBIS vs. their native Indo-Pacific range, what native species occupy the same phylogenetic niche according to the Open Tree of Life, and has anyone studied their invaded-habitat microbiome in MGnify or bioRxiv preprints?* |
| Metabolic disease | *Branched-chain amino acids are elevated in pre-diabetic patients — find the degradation pathways in KEGG, check if the enzyme genes are differentially expressed in diabetic vs. healthy pancreatic islets using Expression Atlas and GTEx, look up metabolite biomarkers in HMDB, and find drug modulators through DGIdb and Guide to Pharmacology.* |
| Cancer dependency | *Which genes are essential for pancreatic cancer cell survival according to DepMap, do any overlap with known drug targets in Open Targets, and are there somatic mutations in those genes across TCGA pancreatic adenocarcinoma samples in GDC and cBioPortal?* |
| Undiagnosed disease | *A child presents with seizures, intellectual disability, and microcephaly — what genes are associated with all three HPO phenotypes simultaneously, which of those genes have pathogenic variants in ClinVar, are any located on chromosome 7q, and what do the Monarch Initiative knowledge graph and Orphanet say about matching rare diseases?* |
| Immunology | *A patient with suspected T-cell lymphoma has a clonal TCR rearrangement — search VDJdb for matching CDR3 sequences, check the IEDB for known epitope specificities, find immune-related gene expression in the ImmPort studies, and look up the associated HLA allele frequencies in gnomAD.* |

### Expert

| Category | Query |
|----------|-------|
| Adverse drug reaction | *A patient on both acetaminophen and isoniazid develops liver failure — could isoniazid's CYP2E1 induction increase NAPQI formation beyond the glutathione detox capacity? Find the enzymes in KEGG and Rhea, check RxNorm for known interactions, look up CYP2E1 population variants in gnomAD with PharmGKB annotations, map the oxidative stress cascade in Reactome, and check if this drug combination has a disproportionate signal in openFDA adverse event data.* |
| Resistance mechanism | *A melanoma patient initially responded to vemurafenib (BRAF V600E inhibitor) but progressed after 8 months — use Ensembl VEP to annotate the V600E variant, find bypass resistance mechanisms in PubMed and Europe PMC, check if those mechanisms involve kinases with existing inhibitors in ChEMBL and Open Targets, examine the structural basis of drug-resistant BRAF in PDB, map the downstream signaling in OmniPath, and search ClinicalTrials.gov for combination trials enrolling patients with acquired resistance.* |
| Gut-brain axis | *The gene SLC6A4 (serotonin transporter) appears in both depression GWAS and gut microbiome studies — find its expression across gut and brain tissues in GTEx and the Allen Brain Atlas, identify gut bacterial metabolites that interact with serotonin pathways via HMDB and MetaboLights, check if SLC6A4 polymorphisms alter transporter kinetics using variant data from dbSNP and ClinVar, search MGnify for microbiome studies involving serotonin metabolism, and look in Semantic Scholar for clinical evidence connecting microbiome composition to SSRI treatment response.* |
| Neoantigen prioritization | *A tumor exome reveals a novel frameshift in TP53 — use Ensembl VEP to predict consequences, check if the mutant region is disordered using DisProt and MobiDB, verify the region has high structural confidence in AlphaFold, confirm TP53 is expressed in the tumor tissue type via GTEx and Human Protein Atlas, search IEDB for known TP53-derived epitopes, look up population HLA allele frequencies in gnomAD, and rule out self-protein homology using UniProt and InterPro.* |
| Cryptic species discovery | *DNA barcodes from a deep-sea vent survey match no known species — find the closest sequences in NCBI Nucleotide, place the organism on the tree of life using Open Tree of Life and TimeTree for divergence estimates, check if the vent location has been surveyed in OBIS and GBIF, identify whether related organisms have characterized metagenomes in MGnify, look for orthologous gene clusters in EggNOG and OMA, and search bioRxiv preprints for recent reports from the same vent system.* |
| Polypharmacy risk | *An elderly patient takes metformin, atorvastatin, amlodipine, and omeprazole — look up each drug in RxNorm and PubChem, map every CYP enzyme involved in their metabolism via KEGG and PharmGKB, find shared enzymes where competitive inhibition could alter plasma levels using STRING interaction data, check pharmacogenomic variants in the patient's background via gnomAD and ClinVar, look up FDA adverse event co-occurrence signals in openFDA for each pairwise combination, cross-reference with DGIdb for drug-gene interactions, and identify which interactions lack clinical guideline coverage in PharmGKB.* |
| Multi-omics integration | *For Type 2 Diabetes, gather gene-disease associations from DisGeNET, identify risk loci from the GWAS Catalog, get tissue-specific expression of top genes from GTEx and Human Protein Atlas, find enriched pathways using g:Profiler and Enrichr, map protein interactions in STRING and Pathway Commons, look up metabolite biomarkers in HMDB and Metabolomics Workbench, check drug-gene interactions in DGIdb and ChEMBL, find rare disease overlaps through Orphanet and HPO, and search for active clinical trials on ClinicalTrials.gov.* |
